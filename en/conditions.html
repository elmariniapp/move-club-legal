The service

MOVE CLUB, published under the name El Marini App, provides walking activity tracking, goals, challenges and ways for members to interact. You can contact the team at support@elmariniapp.com.

Access and accounts

You can use personal tracking in guest mode. Online features may require an account and an internet connection. Provide accurate information when creating your account and keep your login details private. If you need permission from a parent or legal representative to use the service, obtain it before creating an account.

The rules on this page apply when you use the social features. Read and accept them before sending messages or adding content that other members can access.

Respect for members and fair play

Treat others with respect. Harassment, threats, hate speech, impersonation, inappropriate sexual or violent content, spam and sharing someone else’s personal data without permission are prohibited. Only send content you have the right to share.

Do not manipulate step counts, rankings or the way challenges work. Results may be recalculated if the service detects inconsistent data or a rule violation.

Use the reporting and blocking options in the app. You can also contact support. In cases of abuse, access to some features or to the account may be restricted and the relevant content may be removed, depending on the circumstances.

Content you share

You retain your rights over your messages and images. When you choose to send them, you allow MOVE CLUB to host, transmit and display them to the intended recipients to provide this feature. Manage your profile visibility and choose carefully what information you send.

Using MOVE CLUB while walking

MOVE CLUB supports activity tracking and motivation. Step counts, distances, calories and durations may be estimates or vary with your device, settings and signal conditions. They are neither medical measurements nor health advice. Stay aware of your surroundings while walking.

Purchases and subscriptions

When a Premium offer is available, the store shows its price, duration and renewal terms before you confirm. You can manage or cancel your subscription in the store that billed you. Deleting your MOVE CLUB account or uninstalling the app does not cancel the subscription. Your statutory consumer rights continue to apply.

Data and account closure

The privacy policy explains how data is used. The account deletion page explains how to close your account with or without the app.

If these terms change, the current version will be available here. For questions or disputes about the service, contact support@elmariniapp.com.

Request deletion by email

Email support@elmariniapp.com from the address associated with your account if you still have access to it. The button opens a prefilled message in your default email app. If it does not open, use the Gmail link or copy the address into your usual email app.

Lost access to that email address? Tell support that you can no longer access it. We may need to verify your identity to protect the account. Never send a password or sign-in code.

Delete your account from the app

  1. Open MOVE CLUB using the account you want to delete.
  2. In Settings, open Account and choose the deletion option.
  3. Read the summary, choose whether to erase local data as well, and confirm.

What happens when you delete your account

You lose access to the account and profile. The request covers data tied to the account, including relationships, challenge participation and totals, online preferences and notification identifiers. Associated data is handled under the erasure rules and exceptions explained in the privacy policy.

Support cannot remotely erase data stored only on your phone. You can erase it in your device settings or in MOVE CLUB. Manage data held in Health Connect or Apple Health in the relevant service.

If you have a subscription, manage it in the store that billed you. Account deletion alone does not stop subscription renewal.

After you submit your request

We may contact you to verify that you own the account before handling the request. We will let you know what action was taken and whether any data needs to be retained. Account deletion is permanent.

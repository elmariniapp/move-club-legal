Who manages your data

MOVE CLUB is published under the name El Marini App. For questions about data protection or to exercise a right, contact support@elmariniapp.com.

Steps, goals and watches

Step counting can work without an account. The app uses your phone’s activity sensor or, with your permission, step data available in Health Connect on Android. When access to past data is available and authorised, the app uses it to complete your activity record. On iOS, where the integration is available, the app can read steps from Apple Health with your permission.

MOVE CLUB uses step data to show your activity, goals, history, streaks and badges. Your personal tracking history is stored on your device. When you join an online challenge, the service receives the totals needed to calculate the result. Authorised participants can see information such as your display name, your avatar if you added one, and your challenge total.

Reading steps from a health service relies on your explicit consent. You can turn off access in your device settings. Withdrawing consent stops new reads. You can also clear previously stored data in the MOVE CLUB settings.

Walks recorded with GPS

The app uses GPS to record a walk that you choose to start, with your permission. Step counting works without GPS. While a walk is active, the app may continue to record location with the screen off. Route points, times, duration and distance are stored on your device. You can pause, finish or delete a walk.

When a Google Maps basemap is available, displaying it contacts Google and may disclose the area you are viewing. If looking up start and end streets is available, the relevant points are sent to your phone’s geocoding service. These operations are separate from recording the route in MOVE CLUB.

If the “My Walk” share template is available and you choose to include your route, the image may reveal its shape. You choose which app and recipients to share the image with.

Account, interactions and support

  • Account and profile. The service uses your email address, account ID, display name and the information you add to your profile. If Google or Apple sign-in is offered and you choose it, the provider supplies the information needed to identify you.
  • Social features. The online service processes friend requests, invitations, participation, messages and images you send. Recipients and authorised members can see the content you share with them. Reports help us investigate abuse.
  • Notifications. If push notifications are enabled, the service uses an installation or notification identifier to deliver alerts. You can manage notifications in the app or your phone settings.
  • Purchases. If a Premium offer is available, the store processes the payment. MOVE CLUB may use a purchase ID or token and the subscription status to verify access to Premium features.
  • Suggestions and support. We receive the subject, message, reply address if you provide one, and any app version or system information you choose to include. We use these details to handle your request and reply if needed.

Why we use this data

We use the information needed to provide the features you request, including your account, challenges, discussions and purchase verification. Reading steps from a health service and recording GPS rely on your consent, which you can withdraw. Protecting the service and dealing with abuse serve our legitimate interest in securing accounts and interactions. We may also process some data where required by law.

You can use basic tracking in guest mode. Some features require an account, a connection or a permission you choose to grant.

Services that process data

  • Supabase hosts online services, including accounts, social data and challenges.
  • Google and Apple are involved depending on the features you use, including sign-in, app stores, Google Maps and Firebase notifications for Android.
  • Resend delivers suggestions to our support address.
  • GitHub Pages hosts these pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes.

These providers may process some data outside your country. When personal data is transferred outside the European Economic Area, GDPR safeguards apply according to the provider and the transfer. You can contact us for information about the applicable safeguards.

MOVE CLUB adds no data collection form, audience measurement or advertising tracker to this site. An email link opens your email app and the message is sent when you confirm.

Retention and security

Local data is kept for activity tracking according to the applicable history period, until the scheduled purge or until you erase it from the device. Account and service data is kept for as long as needed to provide the relevant features. We retain support requests, reports and technical information as needed to handle them, protect the service and meet applicable obligations.

Data associated with the account is handled when the account is deleted. We may retain some information where required by law or for a legitimate security, fraud prevention or evidentiary need. Access to online data is limited according to the feature and account permissions. Sessions are stored using the operating system’s secure storage mechanism.

Your rights and choices

In MOVE CLUB settings, you can manage permissions and visibility preferences, export your data or erase data on your device, depending on the available options. To delete your account and associated data, follow the account deletion procedure.

Contact support@elmariniapp.com to request access, correction, erasure or restriction, exercise your right to data portability or object to processing where that right applies. You may withdraw consent at any time for processing that relies on it. You may also lodge a complaint with the relevant supervisory authority, such as the CNIL in France.
